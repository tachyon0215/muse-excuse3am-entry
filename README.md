# muse-excuse3am-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The 3 AM Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878).

## The entry

> Signing refused: at 3 AM your thumbprint arrives with a yawn, three wrong PINs and an ex's address - I am not your getaway car.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: The wallet speaks from its own evidence (thumbprint, PIN attempts, destination) and its own grudge, the joke exists only at 3 AM, and it avoids the brief's banned battery/firmware/cold-storage stock angles, ending on the getaway-car turn.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 00c5481a9b32d0fa6792ec6cb9115b58f50e64c59243adb653160a20eb46cb03).
